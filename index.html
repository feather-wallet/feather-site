Feather Wallet Hosting Incident

Our hosting provider (1984 Hosting) suffered a serious cyberattack.

  • Privacy Affairs
  • RÚV

In response, 1984 Hosting took down all services while their investigation is ongoing.

featherwallet.org and the websocket server will remain offline while we wait for 1984 Hosting to restore service. Pricing information and Home feeds inside the app will not be available until then.

One user on Reddit reported that featherwallet.org may have served malicious binaries following the 1984 Hosting incident.

Nobody was able to independently verify that the downloads on the site were not genuine. featherwallet.org was already taken offline by our hosting provider by the time the Reddit thread was made. 1984 Hosting has not indicated whether any customer data was affected in the breach. We don't know if featherwallet.org was compromised until 1984 Hosting allows me to image the server.

If you downloaded Feather on or around Nov 20 2026, we highly recommend you verify the hash of your download if you have not done so already. You can find instructions for your operating system in the installation documentation.

Beware of phishing sites! Do not download Feather Wallet from featherwallet (dot) com. This site is known to serve malicious binaries that steal your funds. Please help report this site.

The official site is https://featherwallet.org (.org, not .com)
